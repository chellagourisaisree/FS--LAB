About Us

ANITS is a private autonomous engineering college established in 2001. It is permanently affiliated to Andhra University, approved by AICTE and accredited with NAAC 'A' Grade. The college offers UG and PG programs with excellent infrastructure and placement opportunities. Visit the Official ANITS Website .

Admissions

Admissions are based on AP EAMCET and JEE Main examinations. More details are available on the Admissions Page .

Academics

The college provides quality education with experienced faculty, modern laboratories, workshops, seminars, and project-based learning.
